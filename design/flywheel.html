Weekly rewards

Every Monday at 12:00 UTC, 10% of the week's protocol revenue is distributed three ways: 40% buys back and burns the three highest-volume tokens, 30% rebates the most active traders, 30% pays outside liquidity providers. Each epoch publishes a manifest listing every transaction, so you can verify every line.

LP attribution v1: positive-liquidity events on token pools, attributed to the transaction sender, protocol addresses excluded. The method is stated in each manifest. If an epoch has no outside LPs, their share is added to the buyback pot.

The reward split is treasury policy v1 — fully transparent in the manifests, not yet enforced by contract.
