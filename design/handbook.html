THE FOUNDER'S HANDBOOK.

doubleplus, from Newspeak: doubleplusgood, the strongest possible good. That is the graduation bar, and this pamphlet is everything behind it.

1 · Found.

One transaction deploys your venture's coin and opens a funding round on a rising price curve: the earlier a backer commits, the cheaper the entry. You swear the full term sheet up front — target, your cut of the raise (to 30%), your vested allocation (to 15%, unlocking linearly only after success), the deadline, the per-wallet cap. It is public before anyone commits and amendable by no one after, including you. Especially you.

2 · Fund.

Backers buy along the curve in plain ETH. Pricing is the exact integral of the curve, so splitting orders or sniping the first block gains nothing, and the cap keeps any single citizen from cornering the round. The arithmetic is disclosed; the trick is that there is no trick.

3 · Graduate.

Hit the target and anyone — you, a backer, a bored keeper — may trigger graduation. Your declared cut of the raise pays out as funding; everything else, remaining supply and remaining ETH alike, becomes protocol-managed liquidity in a Uniswap V4 pool. The DOUBLEPLUSGOOD stamp lands. It is the only stamp the machine gives out gladly.

4 · Earn.

Your fee policy runs on-chain forever: separate buy and sell taxes (0–4% each), split in any proportion across four engines — the dev wallet; ETH dividends pushed to every holder; auto-liquidity locked beside the price; and a market-making engine that quotes both sides — a tight bid wall under the price and an ask band above it, re-centered by keeper when the market drifts. Trades in the first seconds after graduation pay a decaying sniper premium that lands in the walls: snipers fund the book. The protocol adds up to 1% per trade to keep the lights on, and pays a fifth of that to referrers.

5 · Compound.

Share any page with your ?ref= chit: whoever binds it pays you a fifth of the protocol fee on every trade they ever make. Weekly, the flywheel returns a slice of revenue as buyback-burns, trader rebates, and maker rewards, each epoch published as a manifest with the transactions to check. Dividends deliver themselves every fifteen minutes. Raises graduate themselves at target. Refunds open themselves at the deadline. The machine is punctual so that nobody has to be trusted.

6 · Fail, properly.

Miss the deadline and the round fails safe: your allocation burns, and every backer reclaims their full spend by returning their tokens. All or nothing, like a proper raise, with the MEMORY-HOLED stamp for your file and no further correspondence entered into.
